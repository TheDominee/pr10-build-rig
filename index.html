    Ballast rules

    • Ballast safes go in while the scaffold is built; position, orientation and diagonal directions as drawn.
    • Transoms first: close every field with its EV transoms before placing any ballast. They are part of the scaffold's structural integrity.
    • 32 cm steel decks in ballast bays: 4 in bays with ballast safes, 5 in all other ballast bays.
    • Place ballast as far to the sides as possible; leave ≈500 mm free where side wall keders go and two steel decks out per field.
    • Ballast must be able to activate; all ballasted uprights secured against pulling out.
    • Front reinforced towers: BS-VS-2M-HD under the two centre front ballast safes.
    • Manual V1: 8 ballast safes, all at the towers (BS44-207-001/002 at the PA wing towers, BS44-207R-003…008 at the main towers). Front ballast is no longer in a bay in front of the front towers.

        Conditions for safe use

        • Lift the roof only without live loads; load after it is fixed at height (dead hang and cable guys).
        • Tower fixation front to back; maximum spindle length per the spindle table.
        • Only original Eurotruss parts; Layher at least K2000+. Level subsoil with enough bearing capacity.
        • Ballasted uprights secured against pulling out; no connection may work loose.
        • Canopies tensioned against ponding; build only in snow-free periods. Spreader attachment bolts 30 Nm.

        Hoists & rigging

        • Stage 6 × 1 t (11 m lift, 27 m chain); wings 2 × 2 t (16 m lift). Chainmaster, CEE 400 V 63 A. Tell Eurotruss if you use other hoists.
        • Hoists inside the TT truss, chain bag outside. Hook to the opposite truss with a round sling or to a hook attachment plate; delta plates (SL-SB44-HAS) where marked.
        • Hoist to sling: 3.25 t shackle to 4.75 t shackle; hook to 4.75 t shackle. Rope in each tower before erecting.
        • Rear towers: chain wheels close to the tower on the rear side, back wheel one hole forward.
        • 4 × 12 mm × 30 m rope for roof covers and chain hooks.

          What to include

          The export follows the Layers menu and Build mode: whatever is visible in 3D is exported, one DXF layer per 3D layer. Tubes and parts are exported as simple boxes to keep files workable; sheets as faces; cables as lines (DXF only). DXF and MVR come inside one .zip (this page can only hand over zip, pdf and a few other file types). Unzip and import the .dxf or .mvr. The rig plan contains hanging points with IDs, every truss and PA array as 3D boxes, chains, and load labels per point. MVR files have been checked for structure here but not opened in Vectorworks yet.
